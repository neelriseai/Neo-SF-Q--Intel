# Hybrid candidate-fusion foundation review

## Scope

This review covers the isolated A4 deterministic candidate-fusion foundation. It does not claim
real retrieval adapters, ChromaDB persistence, provider execution, workflow consumption, ranked
retrieval quality or release authority. The governance release interlock remains unchanged.

## Independent challenge result

The genericity lane challenged channel self-assertion, exact/lexical conflation, semantic flooding,
evidence-to-entity binding, duplicate amplification, conflict truncation, source neutrality and
scope truth. The governance lane challenged candidate promotion, upstream-gap laundering,
request/query/channel/evaluation roots, freshness with empty channels, paired rehashes, unsafe
diagnostic text and deterministic behavior during semantic outages. Both lanes rejected the first
cut. All P0/P1 findings were corrected and independently rechecked before approval.

## Implemented boundary

The fusion service now accepts bounded, typed exact, lexical, confirmed-graph and semantic
candidate receipts. It binds the project, request, query, snapshot, raw and normalized graphs,
ontology, source profile, context pack, producer, corpus, index, freshness, fusion policy and
evaluation identities. Weighted reciprocal-rank fusion is deterministic; exact and confirmed-graph
admission is protected from optional semantic corroboration. Evidence is bound to allowed context
entities, duplicate logical claims cannot suppress peers, conflicts are atomic, omissions and
outages are explicit, and every result is analysis-only with no authority or release-evidence flag.
Trusted validation recompiles the result against independently supplied roots.

## Truthful foundation gaps

The capability remains `FOUNDATION`. Real exact, lexical, graph and semantic adapters, transitive A3
verification receipts in the service, the ChromaDB persistent adapter and an adjudicated hybrid
quality corpus remain planned. The evaluation artifact explicitly records zero of twenty required
adjudicated cases and therefore supports contract identity/invariant review only, not ranked-quality
claims. A future adapter contract must provide a versioned canonical assertion digest; fusion will
not guess at business-specific case, Unicode or structured-value normalization.

## Verification expectation

The full repository gate must pass after regenerating `knowledge/project-index.json` and
`knowledge/application-graph.json`. The deferred-operator queue records environment/session gates
without storing secrets or pausing unrelated roadmap work.
