# Graph context-pack foundation review

## Scope

This review covers the isolated A3 deterministic context compiler. It does not authorize release
decisions, provider-backed specialists, source-fragment promotion, persistent semantic retrieval or
workflow consumption. The governance 2.x interlock remains unchanged.

## Independent challenge result

The genericity lane challenged demo coupling, resource bounds, deterministic selection, policy
roots, serialized-pack tampering and scope truth. The governance lane challenged false evidence,
candidate promotion, project/snapshot drift, blocking-gap removal and release-authority leakage.
Both lanes initially rejected caller-self-hashed text as authoritative and demonstrated paired
rehash attacks against the first pack schema. Those findings were corrected before approval.

## Implemented boundary

The compiler now produces an analysis-only, immutable, deterministic pack with replay-validated
canonical structure; unresolved source fragments and semantic hits remain candidate-only. It binds
the request, source graph, normalized graph, ontology, source profile, reasoning/evaluation,
propagation and compiler-policy identities. Atomic selection and explicit omission accounting are
bounded by a pinned policy across graph inputs, propagation artifacts, fields, content and the full
serialized envelope. A public trusted validator recompiles from independently supplied roots and
rejects a merely self-consistent rehash.

## Truthful foundation gaps

The pack remains `FOUNDATION`. It cannot report complete analysis because A2 freshness and typed
risk-factor receipts are not implemented. Caller text stays unresolved until a governed artifact
resolver verifies bytes and extraction provenance. Seed-rationale receipts, deterministic
depth/work-unit accounting, provider token/time controls, prior outcomes, semantic-index receipts
and workflow/provider consumers remain planned and have explicit acceptance boundaries in the
scope manifest.

## Verification expectation

The full repository gate must pass after regenerating `knowledge/project-index.json` and
`knowledge/application-graph.json`. Generated knowledge remains discovery context, never authority.
